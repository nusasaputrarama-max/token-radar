## Findings re-reviewed

### P2 — Board gate watchlist eligibility: source-level fix present

In lib/model.ts, boardIssue() now independently checks every watchlist slug for snapshot membership, pre-token status, isRankable(), and TVL at or above snapshot.floor_usd. The existing gate also checks unique board slugs, exact eligible-set coverage, early-row count, rank-to-row-position binding, descending implied values, and slug-ascending tie-breaks.

The new test builds a valid board and then checks that the gate rejects crafted CEX, versioned-name, and below-floor watchlist members. This directly addresses the v2.8 gap where buildBoard() enforced rankability/floor but boardIssue() did not.

**Assessment:** fixed in source and covered by new negative tests on the PR branch. Test execution was not independently verified in this run.

### P1/P2 — Farm-plan chain balance and capital wording: source-level fix present

The chain-family entry step now asks the reader to verify what an app supports and says not to move funds until the app shows support. The weekly step now re-checks the app and no longer says to use an app while keeping a small balance active.

A new test iterates all 28 family fixtures, scans all six rendered steps for small-balance wording, checks every cost field containing a dollar amount for the non-recommendation label, and asserts the chain-specific entry/weekly wording.

**Assessment:** the v2.7 wording finding appears fixed in source and has meaningful regression coverage. Execution and the sealed 226-row output remain unverified by this reviewer.
